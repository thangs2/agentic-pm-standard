Overview — what APS is

Problem APS solves

Teams add AI to PM and get one of two failures:

  1. Human router — every ticket waits on the PM/TDM (Ready, clarify, assign). Throughput collapses at one person.
  2. Garbage acceleration — agents invent AC, auto-move boards, and pods pull work that was never defined.

APS is the third path: exception-based Definition of Ready with typed bounces to the sender, auto-Ready only under policy, and pods that pull without waiting on the PM.

Two gates (never merge)

Gate Question Owner
Quality (DoR) Is this artifact good enough to start? Rubric + agent; sender fixes red; PM on yellow
Pull Will we take it into WIP now? Pod — never waits on PM for permission to pull Ready

APS vs generic “AI PM tips”

Generic internet APS
“Use AI to write user stories” Stories must pass rubric + cites; uncited-claim never auto
“Automate Jira” Allowed transitions are a published set; unauthorized Ready is audited
“PM reviews everything” PM reviews exceptions + sample of greens
“Chat is the spec” Chat is working memory; verified store is SoT
