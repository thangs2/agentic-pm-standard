APS story spine (15-minute read)

North star (say this out loud):

When AI reads, classifies, and drafts delivery work under published policy, people own exceptions, sampling, and the rule—not every ticket in the inbox.

Short tagline for slides and CV: Exception-based readiness for agentic delivery—not a PM mailbox.

Act 1 — The wrong default (2 min)

Teams adopt AI for intake and documentation. Within a month, the PM becomes a mailbox: approve every draft, fix every Jira field, chase every “almost Ready” story.

Symptom: Chat and agents move tickets; evidence (docs, CI, rubric) does not. “Done” in the tracker without a verify log is theater.

APS bet: Automate GREEN paths only. RED returns to the sender with a typed reason. PM time goes to exceptions, sampling, and changing the rule—not retyping acceptance criteria for the hundredth time.

Law: ../06.laws/01.immutable-law.md

Act 2 — One day, one slice (8 min)

Cast: Sender (BA), PM seat, pod lead, agent (classify + draft), tracker.

Beat What happens APS anchor
1 Meet ends; transcript exists Trigger §1b · Case 12
2 Agent drafts MoM + action JSON; no auto-Ready Plane A · Intake only
3 PM publishes MoM; stubs land in Intake Gate rubric
4 Case 11: hierarchy + DoR; RED → sender Case 11
5 Slice Go/No-Go; one primary service/app 01.flow/08.lifecycle/03.slice/02.go-nogo.md
6 Pod pulls Ready → In Progress (plane B) Flow: ready-pull
7 Verify PASS → Human In Review (not agent Done) Factory
8 Optional B′ QC after qualified DEV Done QC lane

You do not need every folder to live this story once. You need one end-to-end slice in your head, then map shards as appendix.

Act 3 — What changes Monday (3 min)

  1. Publish classify + DoR rubric (even v0.1)—GREEN is defined, not vibes.
  2. Route RED to sender with artifact name, not chat shame.
  3. One slice at a time: Go/No-Go, WBS row per file, verify log before HIL.
