Portfolio lens (Hiring Manager · 90 seconds)

Use this shard when APS + CV are the artifact—not when the reader will study the full wiki.

North star: When AI reads, classifies, and drafts delivery work under published policy, people own exceptions, sampling, and the rule—not every ticket in the inbox.

F-pattern scan (what HM sees)

Zone Content Length
Headline Role + outcome (CV) 1 line
Proof strip 3 bullets with metric or named artifact 3 lines
System One diagram: funnel + planes A/B/B′/C 1 visual
Case One deep link (playbook or slice) 1 URL
Fit “In your org I would…” tailoring 5 lines

Do not send HM to ../00.index.md without a story link. Send: story spine → one case → one proof.

“In your org” (tailoring paragraph)

Answer in five sentences:

  1. Intake channel today (email, Jira, meet, IDE).
  2. SoT for AC (doc, tracker, CI).
  3. Where GREEN stops (classify only vs auto-Ready).
  4. Who owns RED (sender vs PM).
  5. First slice you would run (one service/app, one verify command).

Profile hints: ../06.laws/03.tailoring-profiles.md
