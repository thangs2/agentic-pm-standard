Agentic PM Standard · v2.0

Delivery where the agent drafts, and people own the exceptions.

APS is a delivery standard for teams that let an agent read, classify and draft the work under published policy. GREEN work moves on its own; RED work never leaves the sender's court. People stop triaging every ticket and start owning the exceptions, the sampling, and the rule.
